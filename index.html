    Reading the board
    • A note

      Play it when its front edge reaches the bone line. The number is how many holes to cover (0 = all open).

    • High octave (+)

      Same fingering, blow a bit harder.

    • Short note

      Less than a beat.

    • Held note

      Keep blowing; the tail fills up while you sustain it.

    • Green glow

      You hit it. A held note stays lit while you sustain it on pitch.

    • Red glow

      Missed. The skull and number beside a note count how many times it has cost you.

    • Same note again

      Re-tongue it ("tu"), shown by a bright bar along its front edge.

    • Unusual fingering

      For c♮ and half holes ("½"), the little chart above the note shows the six holes, mouthpiece at the top. Filled = covered, half = half covered, ring = open.

    Paste a tab

    Copy the note lines from a tab site (e.g. tinwhistletab.com) and paste them here. Format: d e f# a b d+ c# — + = high octave. Each line becomes a phrase. Every note lasts one beat; add - to hold it longer (d- = 2 beats, d-- = 3) and r for a one-beat rest (r- = 2). Optional bar lines: | between bars, |: … :| around a part played twice, [1 / [2 for endings. You can also paste ABC notation (with its X:, T: and K: lines), e.g. from abcnotation.com.

    Melody from audio
