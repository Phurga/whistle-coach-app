    The single best whistle exercise: play each note for 4 seconds, keeping the needle centred and the sound clear. Steady air = steady pitch. Try to fill every note below.

    Setup

    About 15 seconds of playing that fits the app to your whistle's tuning, its clean and airy sound, and your room's noise. Re-run it if you change whistle, microphone or room.

    Set by setup. Raise it if notes aren't picked up; lower it if background noise triggers notes.

    Settings

    Reading the notes: the bold head is when to play the note (the only thing that's judged). A thin tail is the time until the next note: hold it or keep it short, your choice. A thick tail means the note is written to be held: it fills up while you sustain it, for a "Held!" bonus.

    Keys: Esc settings · ← → move · L listen · P repeat loop. A mistake sends you back to the start of the loop: play its first note to go again.

    Paste a tab

    Copy the note lines from a tab site (e.g. tinwhistletab.com) and paste them here. Format: d e f# a b d+ c# — + = high octave. Each line becomes a phrase. Every note lasts one beat; add - to hold it longer (d- = 2 beats, d-- = 3) and r for a one-beat rest (r- = 2). You can also paste ABC notation (with its X:, T: and K: lines), e.g. from abcnotation.com.

    Find a tune

    Search about 20,000 traditional tunes on thesession.org. They're turned into whistle tab, with repeats played out and the key moved to suit a D whistle when needed.
