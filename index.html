    Reading the board
    • A note: play it when it reaches the bone line. The number is how many holes to cover (0 = all open).
    • High octave (+): same fingering, blow a bit harder.
    • Short note: less than a beat.
    • Held note: keep blowing; the tail fills up while you sustain it, for a "Held!" bonus.
    • Same note again: re-tongue it ("tu").
    • Unusual fingering (c♮, half holes "½"): the little chart above the note shows the six holes, mouthpiece at the top. Filled = covered, half = half covered, ring = open.

    Paste a tab

    Copy the note lines from a tab site (e.g. tinwhistletab.com) and paste them here. Format: d e f# a b d+ c# — + = high octave. Each line becomes a phrase. Every note lasts one beat; add - to hold it longer (d- = 2 beats, d-- = 3) and r for a one-beat rest (r- = 2). Optional bar lines: | between bars, |: … :| around a part played twice, [1 / [2 for endings. You can also paste ABC notation (with its X:, T: and K: lines), e.g. from abcnotation.com.
